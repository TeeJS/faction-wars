# Approved 1939 replacements — installed 2026-10-04

The user approved all five comparison previews on 2026-10-04; all five are now installed. approval-comparison.png shows the current designs on the left and proposals on the right. The original card frame and captions are retained. New flags use a balanced 240x120 rectangle (2:1), with actual transparency in Indochina's swallowtail notch.

Burma, Ceylon and Malta: British Blue Ensigns with local badges; these replace local/national designs with explicit user approval. Malta's shield has no later George Cross. Kazakhstan: 1937–1940 lettering QAZAQ SSR / КАЗАХСКАЯ ССР and period hammer/sickle, no star. Indochina: 1923–1949 French Indochina civil/naval ensign, yellow with French canton and swallowtail; an ensign for the combined region, not a national flag.

All five were redrawn using built-in OpenAI ImageGen in Joe Palooka / Ham Fisher comic ink style. The Burma derivative is offered under CC BY-SA 3.0, Ceylon under CC BY-SA 4.0; original attribution is retained above and in manifest.json. The other three references are marked public domain on their source pages. Downloaded PNG references and their SHA-256 hashes are recorded; SVG SHA-1 values are only recorded where the SVG was successfully downloaded and checked. No Nazi symbols are included.

Approved game cards are installed. The 37x37 sprites and original generator source data remain unchanged because this batch covers Encyclopedia cards in art/planets only. Card source overrides are recorded in ../approved-card-sources.json and separate pack-credit entries.
